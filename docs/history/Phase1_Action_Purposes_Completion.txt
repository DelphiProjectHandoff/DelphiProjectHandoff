LIMITS
Business purposes and actual operating results cannot be inferred from captions
or event names. A knowledgeable contributor must supply project-specific facts
and a named reviewer must check them. Saving/reviewing prose is not proof that
the selected application ran or produced the expected business outcome.
Target identity uses source and parent-local object paths; renamed or moved
targets are not silently relinked. Captured inherited/runtime differences retain
their existing evidence qualifications. Independent human acceptance remains
separate from automated implementation checks.
